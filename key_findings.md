# Key Findings – SWYNEX Task 3

These findings align with the seven insights documented in the Task 2 Exploratory Data Analysis workbook. They were checked against the 120-row cleaned dataset used to build this dashboard. Amounts are shown without a currency symbol because the source data does not specify one.

1. **Dataset coverage:** The dataset contains **120 records**, covering **9 countries** and **12 cities**, from **1 September 2025 to 25 June 2026**.
2. **Overall amount:** Total Amount is **13,613.31**; the average is **113.44** and the median is **116.12**. The mean is **2.68** below the median.
3. **Country contribution:** The **United Kingdom** has the highest total Amount at **3,638.84** across **31 records**, representing approximately **26.7%** of the overall total.
4. **Monthly pattern:** **September 2025** has the highest monthly total (**2,204.43**, 17 records), while **February 2026** has the lowest (**694.76**, 8 records).
5. **Monthly record volume:** September 2025 has the most records (**17**); February 2026 has the fewest (**8**). Monthly totals should be interpreted alongside record counts.
6. **City pattern:** **Manchester** has the highest city total (**2,196.89**) across **19 records**. Total amount and average amount describe different aspects of city performance.
7. **Outlier check:** Using the **1.5×IQR** rule, the lower boundary is **-87.18** and the upper boundary is **303.87**. **No records** fall outside these boundaries.

## Interpretation note
These are descriptive findings from the supplied sample dataset. They describe only the records provided and should not be treated as evidence of wider customer or market behaviour without additional context.
